# Demo credits

`page-*.png`, `demo.json` and `photo_locations.csv` are generated by `npm run demo:generate`. The traveller, dates and officer numbers are fictional; airports, border checkpoints and cities are real public places.

The `sample-*.jpg` pages are crops of photos from Wikimedia Commons. Every crop shows a single stamp or visa page only; serial, receipt and handwritten numbers are covered. `samples.json` holds the stamps that `google/gemini-3.8-flash` read from them (see `docs/model-trial-2026-11.md`).
